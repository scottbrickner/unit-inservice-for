Unit-Level In-Service Content Request

A short planning form for staff proposing a unit in-service — keeps content focused, objectives measurable, and delivery inside a 5–8 minute microlearning window.

This form is a content-planning tool, not a Clinical Ladder submission. If this in-service is also going toward Clinical Ladder credit, you still need the separate Standardized In-Service Approval Form signed by unit leadership per the CL Guidebook — that process is unchanged and this form does not replace it.
After you submit, check your email. You'll receive an automatic message requesting a copy of your presentation, article, slides, or job aid — just reply to that email with the file attached, or upload it to the shared folder linked in that message.
